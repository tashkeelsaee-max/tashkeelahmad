🍉 Juicy Slice – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026 · Developer: Tashkeel Ahmad

Juicy Slice is a casual fruit-slicing game. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information (only if you create an account): your email address, a username you choose, and your password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.
  • Game progress: best score, highest level, number of games played, fruits sliced, and which knife you selected.
  • On-device settings: sound, music and voice preferences are stored only on your device.

If you play as Guest, no account information is collected and your progress stays on your device.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in and keep your progress across devices.
  • To show the worldwide Top Players leaderboard. Only your username and best score are shown publicly — never your email.
  • To send a password-reset email when you request one.

Services we use

Accounts and game progress are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google LLC. See Firebase privacy information. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

What we don't do

  • No ads and no ad tracking.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account data until you delete your account. You can delete your account and all its data at any time — see how to delete your account.

Children

Juicy Slice is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions? Contact us using the developer email shown on the Juicy Slice page in the Google Play Store.
