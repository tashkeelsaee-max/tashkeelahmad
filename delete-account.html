🗑️ Delete your Juicy Slice account

Juicy Slice · Developer: Tashkeel Ahmad

Option 1 – Inside the game (instant)

  1. Open Juicy Slice and log in.
  2. On the home screen, scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and tap DELETE FOREVER.

Option 2 – Ask us

If you can't open the game, email the developer address shown on the Juicy Slice Google Play page, from the email you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Juicy Slice account". We will delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your login (email and password)
  • Your username and leaderboard entry
  • Your scores, levels, fruits sliced and unlocked knives

Nothing is kept after deletion. Game settings stored only on your phone are removed when you uninstall the app.
